Why ambient noise works

Steady sound raises the background level, so sudden noises stand out less.

Sleep

Rain, brown noise and gentle waves cover traffic, snoring and neighbours.

Focus

Pink noise or a café murmur blocks conversations without pulling attention.

Babies

White noise echoes the steady whoosh a baby hears before birth.

Tinnitus

Soft noise can make ringing less noticeable. Ask your doctor about it.

Questions

Is Ambient Noise free?

Yes. The sound machine is free to use in any modern browser, with no account or download.

What's the difference between white, pink and brown noise?

White noise has equal energy at all frequencies and sounds hissy. Pink noise is softer in the highs, like steady rain. Brown noise is deeper still, like a low rumble or distant surf. Read the full comparison.

Can I leave sounds playing all night?

Yes, at a low volume. You can also set a sleep timer from 15 minutes to 8 hours; the sound fades out over the last minute.

Where do the nature sounds come from?

They stream from the Internet Archive (archive.org). You can also search the Archive from the site and add any recording to your mix.

Keep the volume low. Loud sound can damage hearing, especially over hours. Don't put speakers right next to a baby's ears. If you hear ringing or have trouble hearing, stop listening and see an audiologist or doctor.
